# MINDMAKE X3.0

**A second brain your AI operates, with you in control.**

I have ADHD and dyslexia. I built MindMake so I could come back after a distraction without rebuilding the whole picture.

MindMake helps your AI keep track of your projects, decisions and next steps in files you own. The AI carries the remembering and filing; you review its work and choose what happens next.

**MindMake meets the brain you actually have.**

## What it helps with

- **“Where was I?”** Keeps the main task and a next step, including return points after a side trip.
- **Repeating yourself.** Saves consequential decisions and commitments with their sources for later chats.
- **Filing.** Gives your AI an inbox and filing rules so you can hand over rough material.
- **Corrections.** Adds a correcting memory rather than silently rewriting the earlier record.
- **Trust.** Requires evidence for completion claims and permission for consequential actions. AI can still make mistakes; check important results.

Use one workspace for your life, separate ones for businesses, or a personal assistant that keeps track of several ventures. You choose the boundaries. Your working context stays in files you control and can take to another suitable AI app.

## Honest limits

This is an early **public beta**. Its reliability across real projects and fresh chats is still being tested.

Built and checked on a Mac; the assistant workflow has been tried on Claude. Other AI apps and platforms need their own real-world tests. A plain chat window without folder access cannot operate the workspace.

Python 3.9+ runs the checks and task tools; the optional personal MCP needs Python 3.10+. Apple Reminders requires a Mac. Without Python, an AI with file access can still provide simpler file-based help.

Nothing in the folder runs between chats by itself. Reminders and any remote or background services require separate setup and verification. Use one AI writer at a time.

The Python checks and targeted failure tests passed. A complete real migration followed by a fresh-chat resume is the next beta test.
