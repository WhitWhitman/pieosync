# MINDMAKE 3.0

MindMake is a portable workspace designed to help your AI assistant keep track of your larger projects, your decisions, and what comes next.

It grew out of a practical need: being able to return to complicated work after a distraction or interruption without rebuilding the whole picture every time.

## A brain for you and your work

You can keep a MindMake workspace for your personal life, a separate one for a business, or a personal assistant that helps you keep track of several ventures. You choose what belongs together and what stays separate.

The aim is to give your assistant enough working context to understand where you are heading, help you stay focused, and pick up the next useful step.

## Your context stays with you

Your notes, decisions, and project records live in files you control. You can bring that workspace to a compatible AI app, so your working context can move with you between services.

MindMake is designed to help with remembering, organizing, and following through while you remain in charge.
